# D-02R acceptance — Editorial Construction Space and typed plural records

Status: **accepted research hypothesis / not an ontology**  
Owner decision: **D-02R option A**  
Date: **2026-09-04**  
Implementation: **blocked**  
Dataset research: **held**

This record accepts the research recommendation in [`D-02-SEMANTIC-CENTER-REVIEW.md`](D-02-SEMANTIC-CENTER-REVIEW.md). It does not retroactively convert that decision packet into a production specification.

## Accepted hypothesis

Phase-2 research will test:

1. an **Editorial Construction Space** with constructive, referential, and pragmatic/governance axes as a completeness scaffold;
2. a **typed plural record vocabulary** preserving distinctions among artifact state, goals, norms, obligations, assertions, findings, evidence, targets, operations, actors, authority, decisions, outcomes, verification, releases, and episodes;
3. `Gap` as a candidate derived adverse/unresolved view;
4. `EditorialEpisode` as a candidate causal and evaluative envelope.

## Cross-axis rule

The axes are not partitions. One editorial datum may participate in several axes and relations at once.

For example, an API-signature mismatch may simultaneously be:

- an assertion produced by a checker;
- evidence about a documented claim;
- an adverse finding under a parity obligation;
- the result of an observation operation;
- an object in a reviewer dialogue;
- a release-blocking condition under a named authority.

The framework must preserve those simultaneous roles without duplicating the datum into incompatible truths.

## Representation remains open

Acceptance does not decide whether the vocabulary is represented as:

- entities or records;
- facts or fluents;
- typed events;
- transaction contents;
- graph relations;
- materialized projections;
- a combination of these.

D-03 now explicitly researches that boundary. [`D-03-EVENT-STATE-REVIEW.md`](D-03-EVENT-STATE-REVIEW.md) tests whether typed events and multidimensional state give a better representation than the provisional three-layer language.

## Falsification conditions

The accepted hypothesis must be narrowed or rejected if paired-proof experiments show that:

- distinctions cannot be applied consistently across technical reference and prose;
- cross-axis classification produces uncontrolled ambiguity;
- one datum cannot retain simultaneous dimensions without duplication;
- the vocabulary adds types that never change evaluation, authority, history, or action;
- a smaller model preserves all required counterexamples;
- event/state representation forces loss of exact source fidelity;
- disagreement, uncertainty, waiver, or branch divergence collapses into one truth value.

## Non-conclusions

This decision does not require RDF, OWL, SHACL, XML, JSON-LD, a graph database, event sourcing, a finite-state machine, a class hierarchy, or a universal base table. It does not authorize implementation or dataset research.
