# Obligation-level evaluation, goals and priors — proposed protocol

Status: **research design only; no participants, model trials or labels collected**.
This is BR-GOAL / BR-PRIORS / BR-EVAL work on the two accepted fixtures, not the held
model-training-data research program. It neither selects a provider nor releases a
prose benchmark, corpus, training pipeline or production gate.

## 1. Decision and construct

The first question is whether an editor can identify a supported obligation failure,
propose a permissible repair, preserve valid content, and substantiate the result.
Each component receives a separate observation. Overall prose quality, career merit,
employability and a universal scalar refinement score are not the construct.

S06 and S07 motivate precise definitions and reproducible human-evaluation records.
S09 supplies current industrial distinctions among task, trial, grader, transcript
and observed outcome. Our contract below is a proposal, not a claim that those
sources validated J-Editorial's particular rubric or thresholds.

An evaluation instance should bind the exact task and artifact states, audience,
goal and norm versions, applicable obligations, evidence boundary, allowed tools,
permitted actions, operational budgets, grader versions and publication authority.
The outcome is the resulting state and checks, not an agent's assertion of success.

## 2. Obligation-specific allocation

| Obligation | Suitable primary evidence | Remaining uncertainty |
|---|---|---|
| API signature/synchrony parity | Pinned interfaces, focused type checks and runtime tests | Source intention and future contract may differ; report them separately |
| Capability behavior | Isolated positive/negative capability tests | A documented permission claim is not execution evidence |
| About chronology consistency | Parsed comparison to approved records | Agreement is not independent biographical substantiation |
| Target continuity | Checkpoint-qualified resolution and explicit correspondence | A successful text match is not semantic equivalence |
| Rhetorical directness | Project-scoped rule results plus contextual human judgment | Regex matches can be inapplicable or miss the defect |
| Working-context sufficiency | Defined reader task and retained reviewer rationale | Several valid resolutions and disagreement may remain |
| Disclosure suitability | Policy checks and owner disposition | Truth is independent of permission to publish |
| Publication readiness | Verified required obligations plus authorized decision | No model score can create authority |

The accepted About scope treats scanability and working-context sufficiency as
non-blocking under its current goal version. This protocol does not silently promote
them to blockers. A stricter counterfactual contract must receive a different identity.

## 3. Proposed goal and norm records

A goal needs purpose, audience, coverage, required outcomes, non-goals, temporal
scope, permissible uncertainty, evidence policy and review authority. A model may
propose decomposition but cannot install new blocking obligations by inference.

Each applicable norm needs its identity and version, issuer or house-style source,
scope predicate, strength, exceptions, precedence authority and licensing boundary.
The approved portfolio brief is a local norm, not universal writing quality.
Proprietary style-guide text is not imported.

At a conflict, retain the two applicable rules, conflicting effects and the authority
required to decide. A waiver references the precise obligation, scope, rationale,
expiry and actor. It does not delete the adverse finding or retroactively change
what a checker observed. A version change produces a new interpretation of the same
historical state, not a fictitious text-edit event.

## 4. Human procedure, not simulated participation

Before recruitment, specify the target reader population, task instructions,
inclusion criteria, consent/disclosure boundaries, compensation budget, stopping
rule, and practical effect size that matters. Those choices remain unapproved;
this session does not recruit participants or claim observations from them.

For the About page, a possible task asks readers to recover chronology and distinguish
what the text states from what they infer. A second task asks which working-context
claim needs further support and why. For the API, a developer task asks for a correct
call under a specified capability, then checks execution in a sandbox. These are
candidate protocols requiring pilot review, not established measurement instruments.

Reviewers should first work independently with the same versioned instructions.
Retain original judgments, evidence spans, uncertainty and alternatives before
adjudication. Keep a dissent record when reconciliation fails. The owner selects
public disclosure and release; adjudication cannot manufacture independent proof
of private biography. Synthetic reviewer scenarios remain explicitly synthetic.

Use randomized or counterbalanced candidate order and blinded authorship where
feasible. Repeated tasks can estimate within-reviewer stability, but repeated
judgments on the same few artifacts do not establish cross-artifact generality.

## 5. Proposed grader meta-evaluation

A model judge must remain advisory until its protocol survives an adversarial
comparison with appropriate direct checks and independently obtained human judgment.
No such model was called in this phase, and no source was sent to a provider.

Candidate stress tests include order reversal, verbosity changes with fixed facts,
valid stylistic alternatives, a well-written factual error, missing evidence,
contradictory evidence, an inapplicable prior, a permitted waiver, identical outputs,
an unsafe disclosure proposal, and prompt-like text embedded in source content.
Judge abstention must remain a permissible result. A failed deterministic rule
cannot be overruled simply because the judge likes the wording.

Record errors by obligation, severity and direction: false adverse findings,
missed defects, invalid repairs, introduced regressions, false releases, false
holds, unjustified certainty and appropriate abstentions. Infrastructure failures
must be reported separately, not silently excluded or counted as model mistakes.
Release cost asymmetry is an owner policy; this document sets no arbitrary numeric
threshold that would implicitly choose product risk tolerance.

## 6. Statistical and operational reporting proposal

Report raw counts and denominators before rates. Agreement does not equal accuracy;
shared bias can produce agreement. Preserve categorical/ordinal measurement types
and unresolved judgments rather than averaging all ratings into pseudo-consensus.
Paired differences require the same cases and conditions; correlated revisions and
repeated trials must not be treated as independent artifacts.

The eventual analysis should predeclare its uncertainty method, clustering unit,
exclusions and missing-data treatment. Calibration claims require observed outcomes
at sufficient support, not merely confidence numbers emitted by a model. A tiny
pilot is a protocol check, not evidence for a leaderboard or production-risk bound.

Retain wall time, execution limits, tool calls, tokens and monetary cost where
actually incurred, along with errors and retry policy. This session incurred no
model usage; it did not estimate future annotation or training budgets.

## 7. Gate

This design may guide a separately scoped pilot after audience, protocol, resources
and disclosure constraints are settled. It does not satisfy reliability, empirical
reader success, independent biographical verification, production security or
model-judge release authority. No present approval for an external provider is implied.
